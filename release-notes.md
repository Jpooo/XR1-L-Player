XR1-L Player provides native 1080p60 live preview with stereo HDMI audio for the EVGA XR1 Lite on Linux and Windows.

- Futuristic Play, Settings and Preferences menus with Neon Green, Electric Blue and Crimson Red themes.
- Live video fills the main-menu panel during playback; stopping restores the animated XR1 Lite logo.
- Main menu and Settings controls remain accessible while playing. Volume, mute and appearance preferences are saved.
- FPS Test has been removed. Fullscreen and low-buffering native capture remain.

**Downloads**

- **Linux:** extract the Linux ZIP and allow its AppImage to run as a program. A standalone AppImage is also available.
- **Windows:** extract the entire Windows ZIP and open `XR1-L-Player.exe`; keep its assets and DLLs together.
- `SHA256SUMS` verifies all three downloads. GitHub also provides the complete source archives below.

**Requirements:** Intel i5-6XXX or AMD Ryzen 3 1300X or better; NVIDIA GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 560 XT or above; 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended). **USB 3.0 and a USB 3.0 data cable are required.** Use 1080p60 SDR and HDMI Stereo uncompressed.

Eight Linux automated checks passed, including scaled panel rendering across six video formats. Windows UI/panel tests passed under Wine. These checks do not establish physical capture compatibility with every device or host.
